The problem

Cloud ASR mangles bilingual Japanese speech

01 / SCRIPT

English → katakana garble

Japanese meetings mix in English constantly. Generic ASR force-converts English into katakana and drops the minority language — the transcript becomes unreadable.

02 / PRIVACY

APPI says: don’t send it to the cloud

Under Japan’s Act on Protection of Personal Information, confidential meeting audio often can’t leave the device — ruling out cloud ASR entirely.

Why LFM2.5-Audio

A foundation model that runs on the edge

ARCHITECTURE

Hybrid SSM + attention

Liquid Foundation Model design — efficient long-context processing without the cost of pure attention.

AUDIO-NATIVE

FastConformer + LM

Speech encoder feeds a language model directly — one model hears audio and writes text, no bolt-on pipeline.

~1.5B PARAMS

On-device & private

Small enough to run locally on a laptop / edge device — low latency, no audio ever leaves the machine.

JAPANESE

The -JP variant

A Japanese-specialized base — the right starting point for a bilingual, privacy-first meeting assistant.

Our approach

LoRA where it counts — the audio encoder

RECIPE

r=8 LM + r=32 Conformer LoRA

Frozen base + LoRA (via PEFT) on both the language model and the Conformer audio encoder — encoder rank-32 is what fixed the grounding.

Trained on a synthetic CS / EN / JA corpus balanced with FLEURS real speech, one standard "Perform ASR." prompt. Trained on Hugging Face GPUs.

FROZEN BENCHMARK

A matched forgetting control

Every model scored on the same held-out set: CS-FLEURS (196 code-switched) + FLEURS ja_jp & en_us (200 each) — same sentences/speakers, so monolingual loss is attributable, not domain drift.

Script Accuracy = % of English kept in Latin · plus MER, JA-CER/WER, EN-WER.
